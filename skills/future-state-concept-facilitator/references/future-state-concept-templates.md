## Concept-session agenda

```markdown
## Suggested concept-session agenda

1. Reconfirm scope, current-state findings, and decision frame - 15 min
2. Define the future-state promise - 20 min
3. Agree experience principles - 25 min
4. Map the future-state journey - 45 min
5. Identify required service changes - 25 min
6. Playback open questions, dependencies, and next checks - 10 min
```

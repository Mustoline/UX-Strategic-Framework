# Workshop templates

Use these templates as starting points. Adapt them to the workshop purpose and package context.

## Agenda template

```markdown
## Workshop objective

[One short sentence stating the decision, alignment point, or artifact this session must support]

## Participants

* [Role]
* [Role]
* [Role]

## Agenda

### 1. Opening and objective framing ([time])

* Confirm the purpose of the session
* Confirm what must be decided or clarified

### 2. Context and evidence review ([time])

* Review the strongest known signals
* Separate known facts from assumptions

### 3. Core working session ([time])

* [Main workshop activity]

### 4. Convergence and decisions ([time])

* Summarize what the group agrees on
* Name unresolved issues and tradeoffs

### 5. Next steps ([time])

* Confirm outputs, owners, and immediate follow-up
```

## Facilitation prompt bank

```markdown
* What decision are we actually trying to support here?
* What do we know versus what are we assuming?
* Which users, teams, or touchpoints matter most?
* What would success look like in business terms?
* What constraint is truly fixed, and what can still be changed?
* If we had to narrow scope, what would stay in and what would move out?
* What risk are we accepting if we move forward on this basis?
```

# Artifact patterns

Use this reference to choose the right output type for the task.

## Contents

* Workshop readout
* Evidence baseline summary
* Recommendation summary
* Executive brief
* Proposal section
* Risk and next-step section

## Workshop readout

Use when the team has completed a workshop and needs a concise playback that captures decisions, assumptions, open questions, and next steps.

### Best for

* Sponsor workshop outputs
* Scoping workshop outputs
* Mapping session playback
* Option review playback

### The document should answer

* Why did we run this session?
* What did we align on?
* What remains open?
* What needs to happen next?

## Evidence baseline summary

Use when the team needs a short synthesis of existing evidence before workshops, interviews, or recommendation work.

### Best for

* Package A evidence review
* Package B evidence prep
* Package C evidence prep

### The document should answer

* What do we know with reasonable confidence?
* What is still assumption?
* Which signals matter most to the decision?
* What should be validated next?

## Recommendation summary

Use when the team needs a decision-oriented document that states the preferred direction, the rationale behind it, and the practical next step.

### Best for

* Package A recommendation document
* Package B prioritized recommendation

### The document should answer

* What decision are we recommending?
* Why is this the strongest direction?
* What should happen now, later, or not yet?
* What risks or dependencies remain?

## Executive brief

Use when senior stakeholders need a short strategic summary with clear commercial relevance.

### Best for

* Package C executive brief
* Leadership updates
* Decision-maker readouts

### The document should answer

* What is the service or business problem?
* What value is at stake?
* What are the main investment or sequencing questions?
* What decision should leadership make next?

## Proposal section

Use when the work needs to be framed as part of a commercial proposal or formal statement of work.

### Best for

* Proposal drafting
* Scope, activities, outcomes, and client-involvement sections

### The document should answer

* Why is this work needed now?
* What decision will it support?
* What is in scope?
* What will the client receive?

## Risk and next-step section

Use when the team needs to end a document with a practical handoff into delivery or the next discovery step.

### Best for

* Recommendation documents
* Workshop readouts
* Executive briefs
* Proposal close sections

### The document should answer

* What is still unresolved?
* What could block or weaken the recommendation?
* What should happen immediately next?
* Who needs to act on what?

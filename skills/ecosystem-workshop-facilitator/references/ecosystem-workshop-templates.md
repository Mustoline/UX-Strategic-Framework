```markdown
* [Theme]
```

## Half-day agenda

```markdown
## Suggested half-day ecosystem agenda

1. Reconfirm strategic frame and interview themes - 20 min
2. Map departments, channels, systems, and actors - 50 min
3. Define the service boundary and in-scope study area - 40 min
4. Identify critical service moments and handoffs - 45 min
5. Confirm study focus, risks, and open questions - 25 min
```

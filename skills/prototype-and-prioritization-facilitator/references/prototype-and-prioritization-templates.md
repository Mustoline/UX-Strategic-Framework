## Prioritization-workshop agenda

```markdown
## Suggested 2-hour prioritization agenda

1. Reconfirm the concept, prototype scope, and decision frame - 15 min
2. Review the clickable core flow and key implications - 25 min
3. Agree prioritization criteria - 15 min
4. Rank opportunities and delivery moves - 40 min
5. Review defer and validate-next items - 15 min
6. Playback recommendation, risks, and next steps - 10 min
```

# Roadmap And Business-Case Framer

Use this skill for Package C Activity `4.1`: the final Package C step that turns the validated future-state service model into a practical basis for prioritization, sequencing, and investment discussion.

This skill should help the team identify what should happen now, next, and later, clarify dependencies across teams, systems, and operations, summarize the operating-model and ownership changes required, and connect the recommended direction to adoption, efficiency, support reduction, revenue, retention, or other business outcomes through a credible ROI hypothesis.

This skill should build on the helper layer:

* Use [workshop-design-kit](../workshop-design-kit/SKILL.md) for agenda, facilitation structure, and playback logic
* Use [artifact-writer](../artifact-writer/SKILL.md) to draft the roadmap, change summary, and business-case framing cleanly
* Use [ux-strategy-reviewer](../ux-strategy-reviewer/SKILL.md) as the final quality check on client-facing outputs
* Use [evidence-ingest](../evidence-ingest/SKILL.md) if operational, support, adoption, revenue, or retention inputs need to be normalized into the hypothesis

It should use earlier Package C outputs where relevant:

* [executive-and-service-owner-interviewer](../executive-and-service-owner-interviewer/SKILL.md) for the investment questions, strategic priorities, and risks
* [ecosystem-workshop-facilitator](../ecosystem-workshop-facilitator/SKILL.md) for the cross-functional dependencies and service boundary
* [service-blueprint-builder](../service-blueprint-builder/SKILL.md) for the current-state hotspots, delay points, and ownership gaps
* [future-state-service-model-and-validation](../future-state-service-model-and-validation/SKILL.md) for the validated target-state model, feasibility signals, and refinement outcomes

## What this skill should produce

For most requests, produce a roadmap-and-business-case pack with:

* A roadmap objective tied to the decision the client now needs to make
* A timed agenda for a half-day roadmap and business-case session
* A phased roadmap showing what should happen now, next, and later
* A dependency and decision-point view across teams, systems, and operations
* A change-implication summary covering operating model, governance, and ownership changes
* An ROI hypothesis summary linking the recommended direction to the most credible outcome signals

## Operating modes

### Prep

Before the roadmap and business-case step, confirm:

* Which validated target-state model is being carried forward
* Which strategic or investment decision this step must support
* Which dependencies, feasibility signals, or non-negotiables are already known
* Which outcomes or value areas matter most to the client
* Which stakeholders need to take part in sequencing and investment discussion

Then:

* Use `workshop-design-kit` to shape the half-day roadmap and business-case session
* Use the flow in [roadmap-and-business-case-patterns.md](references/roadmap-and-business-case-patterns.md)
* Prepare the output structure in [roadmap-and-business-case-templates.md](references/roadmap-and-business-case-templates.md)

### Run

During the roadmap and business-case step:

* Keep the discussion tied to the validated service direction rather than reopening earlier concept decisions
* Clarify what must happen now, next, and later and why
* Make cross-functional dependencies, ownership changes, and governance implications visible
* Distinguish near-term delivery moves from larger operating-model changes
* Frame the ROI hypothesis around credible outcome logic rather than false precision
* End with a clear playback of the phased roadmap, the main change implications, and the investment logic

### Synthesize

After the workshop:

* Produce a concise phased roadmap and dependency view
* Summarize the operational, governance, and ownership implications clearly
* Frame the ROI hypothesis using the strongest available value signals
* Highlight the main decisions, assumptions, and risks that still matter
* If needed, hand the draft to `artifact-writer` and then to `ux-strategy-reviewer`

## Core objectives

The roadmap and business-case work should answer:

* What should happen now, next, and later to move from the current model to the future state?
* Which dependencies or decision points shape the sequencing?
* Which operating-model, governance, or ownership changes are required?
* Which outcomes make the investment worth pursuing?
* What is the most credible ROI hypothesis the work can support at this stage?

## Framing rules

* Keep the roadmap tied to the validated service model and the most material dependencies
* Show sequencing as a practical path, not a perfect long-term master plan
* Make ownership and governance changes explicit where they affect delivery or adoption
* Frame ROI as a hypothesis built on credible signals, not as a promise
* Separate what can be acted on now from what still depends on further proof or funding
* Prefer a usable investment narrative over a polished but vague strategy summary

## Minimum output standard

Unless the user asks for something else, return these sections:

1. Roadmap objective
2. Suggested half-day agenda
3. Phased roadmap
4. Dependencies and decision points
5. Change implications
6. ROI hypothesis, risks, and next steps

## Boundaries

This skill covers Activity `C4.1` only. It frames the phased roadmap, change implications, and business-case logic for Package C. It does not replace a detailed program plan, detailed resourcing model, or full financial business case.

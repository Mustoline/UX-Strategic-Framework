## Half-day roadmap agenda

```markdown
## Suggested half-day roadmap and business-case agenda

1. Reconfirm target-state direction, validation signals, and decision frame - 20 min
2. Define what happens now, next, and later - 45 min
3. Map dependencies, decision points, and blockers - 45 min
4. Summarize change implications across teams, systems, and governance - 35 min
5. Frame ROI hypothesis and playback next decisions - 35 min
```

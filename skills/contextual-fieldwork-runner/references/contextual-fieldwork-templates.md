## Contextual guide

```markdown
# Contextual fieldwork guide

## Opening context

* What part of the service are we observing or discussing today?
* What role do you play in this part of the service?

## Walkthrough of the service moment

* Can you show or walk through what happens step by step?
* Which people, systems, or channels are involved?
* Where does the work slow down, repeat, or need extra effort?

## Workarounds and hidden effort

* What do you do to keep things moving when the formal process does not work?
* Where do you need to chase, clarify, escalate, or compensate manually?
* What work is important here but usually invisible?

## Ownership and dependencies

* Where is ownership unclear?
* Which handoffs or approvals create friction?
* Which systems or teams make this harder than it should be?

## Closing

* What is the one thing that most needs to change in this part of the service?
* What should we make sure to study next?
```

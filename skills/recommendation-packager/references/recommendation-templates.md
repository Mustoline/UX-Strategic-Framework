# Recommendation templates

Use these templates as starting points for Package A recommendation packaging.

```markdown

[The decision the sponsor needs to make after the sprint]

[Short statement of the recommended direction]

## Why this direction

* [Reason grounded in evidence, breakdowns, or tradeoffs]
* [Reason grounded in evidence, breakdowns, or tradeoffs]

## Target users and success measures

### Target users

* [Group]
* [Group]

### Success measures

* [Measure]
* [Measure]

## What to build next

* [Immediate next move]
* [Immediate next move]

## What to defer or not build yet

* [Deferred item]
* [Deferred item]
```

# Scoping workshop templates

Use these templates as starting points for Package B scoping workshops.

## Scoping workshop agenda

```markdown
# Scoping workshop agenda

## Workshop objective

[State the journey, the business case, and the decision this phase needs to support]

## Participants

* [Sponsor role]
* [Stakeholder role]
* [Stakeholder role]

## Agenda

### 1. Opening and objective framing (10 min)

* Confirm why this phase is happening now
* Confirm the decisions this work must support
* Confirm what is out of scope for the session

### 2. Business case and commercial context (25 min)

* Clarify the business trigger
* Clarify the cost of moving ahead without enough clarity
* Clarify the outcomes at stake

### 3. Journey scope and stakeholder perspective (30 min)

* Define the journey or service area in scope
* Identify users, teams, and systems that matter most

### 4. Success measures and scope boundaries (25 min)

* Define success for the phase
* Capture in-scope and out-of-scope boundaries
* Note constraints and dependencies

### 5. Discovery questions and research priorities (20 min)

* Name what remains unknown
* Confirm what research needs to clarify first

### 6. Playback and close (10 min)

* Summarize what is agreed
* Summarize what remains open
* Confirm next steps
```

# Scoping workshop patterns

Use this reference to run a focused 2-hour scoping workshop for Package B.

## Contents

* Session objective
* Recommended 2-hour flow

* Follow-up probes
* Common watchouts

## Session objective

The purpose of the scoping workshop is to align the engagement around the right journey, business case, and success frame before research and concept work begin.

By the end of the session, the team should have enough alignment on:

* The journey, product, or service area in scope
* The business case and why the work matters now
* The outcomes the work should influence
* The success measures for the discovery phase
* The questions later research must answer

## Recommended 2-hour flow

### 1. Opening and objective framing: 10 minutes

Focus:

* Confirm why this discovery phase is being considered now
* Confirm what decisions this work must support
* Confirm what the session is and is not for

### 2. Business case and commercial context: 25 minutes

Focus:

* Clarify the business trigger
* Clarify the cost of inaction or poor scope decisions
* Clarify what business outcomes are at stake

### 3. Journey scope and stakeholder perspective: 30 minutes

Focus:

* Define the journey, service area, or product slice in scope
* Identify the users, internal teams, and systems that matter most
* Clarify where the biggest uncertainty or friction seems to sit

### 4. Success measures and scope boundaries: 25 minutes

Focus:

* Define what a useful outcome from the phase would look like
* Clarify what is in scope and out of scope
* Note fixed constraints and known dependencies

### 5. Discovery questions and research priorities: 20 minutes

Focus:

* Name what the team still does not know
* Identify what research needs to clarify first
* Distinguish assumptions from agreed facts

### 6. Playback and close: 10 minutes

Focus:

* Play back the aligned scope
* Confirm the main success measures
* Confirm the priority discovery questions and immediate next steps

## Critical questions by section

### Business case and commercial context

* What business issue or opportunity makes this work relevant now?
* What happens if the organization moves into delivery without more clarity?
* Which commercial, operational, or service outcomes are under pressure?
* What decision must be easier after this phase?

### Journey scope and stakeholder perspective

* Which journey, product area, or service slice should this phase cover?
* Which users, customers, or internal teams matter most?
* Which systems, handoffs, or channels shape the experience enough to be in scope?
* Where do stakeholders currently disagree on what the real problem is?

### Success measures and scope boundaries

* What would make this phase feel successful?
* Which signals would show that the work improved decision quality?
* What is explicitly in scope?
* What is explicitly out of scope?
* Which constraints are fixed, and which are assumptions?

### Discovery questions and research priorities

* What do we still need to learn before delivery scope can be set with confidence?
* Which user or service question matters most?
* Which assumption is currently the most risky?
* What would make us change scope or priority after the research?

## Follow-up probes

Use these when stakeholder answers stay too broad:

* Can you make that more specific?
* Which part of the journey are you referring to exactly?
* How would we recognize improvement in practice?
* Is that in scope for this phase, or simply related to it?
* What decision would change if we learned the answer to that question?
* What is the risk if we leave that question unresolved?

## Common watchouts

* The workshop tries to solve the concept before the scope is clear
* Stakeholders describe a broad transformation instead of a workable journey slice
* The business case stays abstract and cannot guide later prioritization
* In-scope and out-of-scope boundaries are not stated clearly
* Discovery questions are too generic to guide research recruitment and synthesis
* The session ends without a clear playback of the aligned scope

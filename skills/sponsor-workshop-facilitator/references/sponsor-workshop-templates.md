# Sponsor workshop templates

Use these templates as starting points for Package A sponsor workshops.

## Sponsor workshop agenda

```markdown
# Sponsor workshop agenda

## Workshop objective

[State the business question and the delivery decision this sprint needs to support]

## Participants

* [Sponsor role]
* [Decision-maker role]
* [Decision-maker role]

## Agenda

### 1. Opening and objective framing (10 min)

* Confirm the purpose of the sprint
* Confirm the decision this session must support
* Confirm what is out of scope

### 2. Problem framing and business context (20 min)

* Clarify the current pain point or opportunity
* Clarify why this matters now
* Clarify the risk of moving ahead too early

### 3. Target users and journey focus (20 min)

* Identify the users or staff groups that matter most
* Narrow the journey step or workflow in scope

### 4. Success measures and constraints (20 min)

* Define what success looks like
* Capture business and technical constraints

### 5. Open questions and sprint priorities (15 min)

* Name what remains assumption
* Confirm what the sprint must clarify first

### 6. Playback and close (5 min)

* Summarize what is agreed
* Summarize what remains open
* Confirm next steps
```

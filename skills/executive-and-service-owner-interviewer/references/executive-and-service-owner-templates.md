## Interview guide

```markdown
# Executive and service-owner interview guide

## Opening and role context

* How do you see your role in relation to this service or initiative?
* Which outcomes are you most accountable for here?

## Business case and strategic pressure

* Why is this work becoming important now?
* Where is the current service model under pressure?
* What business or service outcomes are most at stake?

## Service breakdowns and operating issues

* Where do you see delays, duplication, support burden, or missed value?
* Where do teams, systems, or channels create friction for one another?
* Where is ownership unclear or hard to manage?

## Risks and tradeoffs

* What is the biggest risk if nothing changes?
* What is the biggest risk if the wrong change is prioritized?
* Which tradeoffs are already visible?

## Investment questions and next decisions

* Which questions need clearer evidence before an investment decision can be made?
* What should this work make easier to decide in the next phase?
```

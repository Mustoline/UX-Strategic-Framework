# Interview invite and consent template

## Purpose

Use this template to invite participants to interviews or contextual sessions in Package B or Package C, and when needed in other packages.

The goal is to explain why the conversation is happening, what participation involves, and what the participant is agreeing to in plain language.

## When to use it

Use this template for:

* user interviews
* customer interviews
* executive interviews
* service-owner interviews
* contextual interviews
* observation or shadowing sessions

## Required structure

Use this order in the message:

1. `Why we are inviting you`
2. `What the conversation is about`
3. `What participation involves`
4. `Consent and confidentiality`
5. `Practical details`
6. `How to respond`

## Reusable draft

### Subject
[Interview invite: <project / activity name>]

### Message
Hi,

We are inviting you to take part in a focused interview as part of a discovery package for this project.

### Why we are inviting you
[Explain why this person or role matters to the work.]

### What the conversation is about
[State the topic in plain language.]

### What participation involves
* A conversation of approximately [Text]
* Questions about your experience, perspective, work, or decision process
* No preparation is required unless we ask for something specific in advance

### Consent and confidentiality
* Your participation is voluntary
* We will use the session to understand patterns, needs, barriers, decisions, or operational realities relevant to the project
* We will not attribute quotes or observations by name outside the agreed project team unless we explicitly agree otherwise
* [Add any recording, note-taking, or observation consent details needed for this project]

### Practical details
* Date and time: [Text]
* Duration: [Text]
* Format: [Remote / in person / observation / shadowing]
* Location or link: [Text]

### How to respond
Please reply to confirm availability or suggest an alternative time.

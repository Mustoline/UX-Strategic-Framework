## Checklist

### 1. Decision readiness

- [ ] The decision this activity should support is clear
- [ ] The activity is bounded enough for the package
- [ ] The success criteria for a useful output are clear
- [ ] The previous stage has been validated

### 2. People readiness

- [ ] The right people are invited or confirmed
- [ ] There is a clear decision owner or sponsor
- [ ] Participants know why they are involved
- [ ] Missing stakeholders or evidence owners are noted as a risk

### 3. Material readiness

- [ ] The relevant project input file in `01-inputs/` is identified
- [ ] The relevant facilitator guide section is identified
- [ ] Any needed evidence, exports, or background material has been requested
- [ ] Scope and out-of-scope boundaries are ready to use in the session

### 4. Facilitation readiness

- [ ] The objective and agenda are ready
- [ ] The critical questions are ready
- [ ] The note-taking or capture approach is clear
- [ ] The handoff back into the AI process is clear

### 5. Risk and feasibility check

- [ ] Known constraints are explicit
- [ ] Key assumptions are visible
- [ ] Any likely blockers are recorded
- [ ] The activity still feels worth running in its current form
